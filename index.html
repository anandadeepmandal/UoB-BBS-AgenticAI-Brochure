The Programme

Transformative Learning,
Grounded in Business Reality

In an era rapidly reshaped by artificial intelligence, strategic leadership demands more than awareness — it demands action. Birmingham Business School's Applied Agentic AI for Business Transformation equips decision-makers with the insights, frameworks, and practical tools to lead AI-driven change across their organisations.

Combining Birmingham's academic rigour with applied industry relevance, this 8-week online programme takes mid-to-senior professionals from foundational AI concepts through to governance, ethics, and the construction of an executive-ready AI adoption roadmap.

Each week features hands-on mini-projects, giving participants a safe, structured environment to prototype AI use cases relevant to their own organisational context — with expert facilitator support throughout.

Programme Structure

Course Outline

1
Foundations of Generative and Agentic AI
  • Generative AI fundamentals and large language models
  • AI chatbots: past, present, and future business applications
  • Cost-optimised models and performance trade-offs
  • Multimedia and language interaction models
  • Advanced applications of Generative AI tools
2
The Rise of Agentic AI and Emerging Platforms
  • Emerging agentic AI platforms and ecosystems
  • Single vs. multi-agent architectures
  • Open-source vs. closed-source AI systems
  • Evaluating platforms for strategic fit and risk
  • Live demonstrations and hands-on exploration
3
Connecting Agents to Digital Ecosystems
  • Building agents into existing enterprise workflows
  • Integrating AI with ERP, CRM, and cloud platforms
  • APIs, data pipelines, and system connectors
  • Edge cases of agent integration in real organisations
  • Empathy and response-tuning for customer-facing agents
4
Agent Risks, Disinformation, and Systemic Impact
  • Classic and emerging cybersecurity risks for AI
  • Cybersecurity considerations for agentic ecosystems
  • Limitations of agent perception and error correction
  • Addressing deepfakes and prompt injection vulnerabilities
  • Reputational and systemic risks of AI deployment
5
AI Agents by Business Function
  • The maturity cycle of agentic implementation (crawl–walk–run)
  • AI in product development: prototyping, QA, and launch automation
  • Functional deployments: HR bots, finance advisors, IT co-pilots
  • Centralised vs. embedded agent architectures in the enterprise
  • ROI measurement and business case development
6
The Last Mile — From Pilot to Practice
  • Voice agents: synthesis, phone systems, and real-time applications
  • Why pilots succeed but deployments stall — and how to fix it
  • Internal resistance and change management strategies
  • Monitoring agent performance: metrics, KPIs, and feedback loops
  • Scaling successful pilots into organisation-wide adoption
7
Governance, Compliance, and Agent Testing
  • Regulatory overview: GDPR, CCPA, HIPAA, and the EU AI Act
  • Testing agent behaviour: sandboxing, A/B testing, safety checks
  • Agent speed vs. oversight: where to insert guardrails
  • Documentation, audit trails, and compliance readiness
  • Building an AI ethics committee and governance charter
8
Strategy Capstone — Leading the Agentic Future
  • Strategic roadmap development: short-, medium-, and long-term
  • Team structure, vendor selection, and internal capability building
  • Organisational culture and leadership for AI adoption
  • Executive presentation: AI adoption plan or strategic pitch
  • Maturity assessment and change management for sustained success

* Modules and curriculum are subject to change. Live examples and case studies are updated each cohort to reflect the latest developments in the field.
